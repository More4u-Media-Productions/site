THE IDEA

One communication layer.

Echo grew from a simple need for communication without the artificial walls between messages, media, calls and everything surrounding them.

Its architecture is designed around real users, realtime synchronization, permission-aware data and a communication experience that can keep growing without losing the original purpose.
