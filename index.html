WHY MORE4U

Build things that make life easier.

More4u Productions is a Canadian youth-driven innovation studio born from a simple idea: build things that make life easier.

After years of experimenting with animation, repair projects, multiplayer games and early creative ventures, More4u became the place where those passions came together. Today, the studio builds apps, products, entertainment and digital experiences that can help people work, communicate, learn and have fun.
