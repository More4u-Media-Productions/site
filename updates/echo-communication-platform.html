AUGUST 2026 · MILESTONE

Echo becomes a complete communication platform.

The project has grown from messaging into a broader communication system.

Echo now brings together text messaging, media sharing, voice messages, direct and group audio/video calling, global search, profiles and presence, notifications and an AI layer.

The milestone represents a shift in what Echo is meant to be: not simply another messaging interface, but a place where different forms of communication can live together.

There is still plenty to build. That's part of the point.
