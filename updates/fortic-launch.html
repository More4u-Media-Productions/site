Fortic launches.

More4u is taking what it has learned from building digital products and using it to help local businesses move forward.

Fortic is a new More4u agency focused on digital elevation for local businesses. It can help strengthen a business's website, local presence, social presentation, review systems, organization and other parts of its digital foundation.

The goal is not to force every business through the same package. An Elevation Agent reviews what a business needs, talks through the situation with the owner and helps shape the work around the business itself.

Fortic is also part of More4u's larger story. More4u started with an ambitious idea and kept building, learning and improving. Fortic is a way to use that experience to help other businesses move forward too.
