ABOUT MORE4U

More for you.

More4u Productions is a Canadian youth-driven innovation studio born from a simple idea: build things that make life easier.

THE COMPANY

A growing technology company.

Founded in late 2024 after years of experimenting with animation, repair projects, multiplayer games and early creative ventures, More4u emerged as the place where those passions finally came together.

Today, More4u builds apps, products, entertainment and digital experiences designed to touch different parts of everyday life. From Echo, built to make communication effortless, to Dayline, designed to simplify productivity and work, to future entertainment projects like TV shows and animations, the studio builds anything that can improve how people work, communicate, learn and have fun.

Its mission is personal: one mission, one connection, one action toward tools that bring real convenience, real connection and real creativity to everyday living.

Guided by the philosophy “More for You,” More4u aims to innovate with purpose, solve real problems and build a lasting ecosystem of meaningful products powered by youth, imagination and the belief that technology can make life better.

THE SPARK

A studio built from curiosity.

More4u is more than a normal company structure. It is a place for ideas to become real, for experiments to turn into products and for useful technology to grow from curiosity.

The name reflects the goal: More for You. More convenience. More connection. More creativity. More possibilities.

ETHAN JERRY

The person behind More4u.

Ethan Jerry is a developer, innovator and founder of More4u Productions. He builds apps, games and digital products with a focus on turning ideas into useful, creative experiences that make life better.

He approaches building as a cycle: imagine it, make it real, find what breaks, improve it and keep going.
