ABOUT MORE4U

More for you.

More4u Productions exists to build things that make life better in practical, creative and sometimes unexpected ways.

THE COMPANY

A growing technology company.

More4u Productions is a technology company creating software, apps, games and digital experiences. Its projects can live in different parts of everyday life, from communication and organization to entertainment and future ideas.

The company's name is its philosophy: More for You. More convenience. More connection. More creativity. More useful tools. More reasons to enjoy the technology around you.

EJ

ETHAN JERRY

The person behind More4u.

Ethan Jerry is a developer, innovator and founder of More4u Productions. He builds apps, games and digital products with a focus on turning ideas into useful, creative experiences that make life better.

He approaches building as a cycle: imagine it, make it real, find what breaks, improve it and keep going.
